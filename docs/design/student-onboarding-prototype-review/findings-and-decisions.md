## Severity guide

- **Critical** - cannot complete a core outcome, or unsafe behaviour.
- **High** - severe hesitation or wrong target, likely to fail the teaching goal.
- **Medium** - friction, but a workaround exists.
- **Future** - polish or preference, deferred with an owner and a reason.

## Decision guide

- **Fix now** - update the prototype or copy before handover.
- **Document** - clarify in the student or contributor guide (TUT-D04).
- **Future backlog** - deferred with an owner and a reason.
