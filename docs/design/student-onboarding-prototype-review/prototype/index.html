Welcome to OnTrack

A short, optional tour shows you four things new students look for. It only shows you where they are and will not change anything for you.

  • selecting a unit
  • the project or task dashboard
  • the target-grade option
  • the Calendar area

Leave the tour?

You can leave now and OnTrack will keep working normally.

You are ready to go

You have seen how to find your unit, the dashboard, the target-grade option and the Calendar area. You can replay this any time from Tutorial and Help in the menu.

We could not find a current unit

You may not be enrolled in a unit yet, or the enrolment information may still be loading. Check that you are signed in to the right account. You can keep using OnTrack normally.

Your progress may not be saved

You can keep going through the tour, but it may not remember where you stopped. OnTrack keeps working normally.
